How to use it at the water

    
    
      
  1. Start the compassPress "Use my phone's compass" and allow location and motion. Your position is used on your phone to work out directions and is not sent to us.
    2. 
      
  2. Hold it up like a cameraPoint the phone at the ship. The ship nearest the centre line is named, and the ones beside it are shown too, so you can see what else is close.
    3. 
      
  3. Line it up oncePhone compasses drift. If a ship you recognise sits off to one side, drag the view until it is centred. The correction is remembered on this phone.
    4. 
    

    
Tip: wave the phone slowly in a figure of eight before you start. It settles the compass. Ships are placed from their radio position reports, which can be a minute or two old.
